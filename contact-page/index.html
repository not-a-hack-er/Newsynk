About Newsynk and our sources

Newsynk aggregates links and article previews from third-party publishers. Newsynk does not report or own those articles. Each article displays its publisher and links to the original story.

Our public RSS fallback uses BBC News and The Guardian. Other sources may appear when their feeds are available. The publisher shown on each story identifies its source.

For a correction to an original article, contact its publisher. For a Newsynk listing, attribution, or technical issue, email us above.
