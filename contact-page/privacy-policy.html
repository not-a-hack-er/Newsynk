Privacy Policy

Newsynk — News Aggregator App

Last updated: April 2026  ·  Developed by Akarsh Bajpai

📋 Overview

Newsynk ("the app") is a news aggregator application that curates headlines from trusted third-party news providers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Plain English summary: We collect only what is necessary for your account and app experience. We never sell your data. We never show ads.

📦 Information We Collect

  • Account data: Email address and display name when you register via email/password or Google Sign-In.
  • Profile data: Avatar color preference and profile photo (stored locally on your device).
  • Usage data: Article bookmarks, votes (upvotes/downvotes), and comments you submit — stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Notification preferences: Which categories you opt into for push notifications (stored locally in SharedPreferences).
  • Device token: Firebase Cloud Messaging token for delivering push notifications (not linked to personal identity).

🚫 What We Do NOT Collect

  • Precise location data
  • Contact lists or phone numbers
  • Camera access or photos (only gallery selection for avatar, on your initiative)
  • Payment or financial information
  • Behavioural tracking or advertising identifiers

🔧 How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account session.
  • To save your bookmarks, votes, and comments so they persist across devices.
  • To send you push notifications for the news categories you select (only with your permission).
  • To personalise your username and avatar within the app.

🔗 Third-Party Services

Newsynk integrates the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database (Firestore), and push notifications.
  • The Guardian Open Platform — news content (The Guardian API).
  • GNews — news content aggregation.
  • Currents API — news content aggregation.

All news articles shown in Newsynk are sourced from the above providers and link directly to the original publisher. Newsynk does not create, modify, or own any news content.

🔒 Data Security

Your account data is stored securely in Google Firebase, which is protected by industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. We do not store passwords — all authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.

🗑️ Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at letnewsynk@gmail.com. We will permanently delete your account data from our Firebase database within 30 days.

👶 Children's Privacy

Newsynk is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

📨 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
